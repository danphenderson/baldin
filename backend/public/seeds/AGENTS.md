# Backend Seed Fixtures Agent Instructions

## Owner

- `baldin_backend`.

## Scope

- Applies to tracked seed fixture data.

## Do

- Keep seeds deterministic, minimal, and safe for local development.
- Document behavior assumptions encoded in seed data.
